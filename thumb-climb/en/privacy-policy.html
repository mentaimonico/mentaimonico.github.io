Privacy Policy

Thumb Climb (iOS / Android app) / Effective: November 17, 2026 / Updated: November 21, 2026 · 日本語

This policy describes how Thumb Climb (the "App") handles user information.

1. Information stored on the device

The App stores your stage progress and records, outfit choices, stamina, number of rescue ropes, your best runs (ghost), total records, sound setting and Premium purchase status on the device. The developer does not receive any of this. A copy of your progress may be saved to iCloud on iPhone, or to the device backup (Google Drive) on Android; these are managed by Apple or Google, and the developer does not receive them. Data on the device is deleted when you delete the App.

2. Game Center (iOS)

Leaderboards use Apple's Game Center. If you are signed in to Game Center, the App sends your clear times and total stars to Game Center and receives your rank. This information is managed by Apple, and the developer does not receive it. Runs that used a rescue rope are not sent.

3. Advertising

The App uses Google AdMob, an advertising service by Google LLC. AdMob may collect the following to deliver and measure ads:

  • Advertising identifier (on iOS, the IDFA, only if allowed through App Tracking Transparency; on Android, the advertising ID)
  • Approximate region estimated from the IP address
  • Interactions with ads, such as views and taps
  • Diagnostic information about crashes and performance

All features of the App work even if you do not allow tracking; ads are then shown without the advertising identifier. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. On Android, you can delete or reset the advertising ID in the device settings.

For how Google handles information, see Google's advertising policies and the Google Privacy Policy.

4. In-app purchases

"Premium" and "Stamina +100" are purchased and restored through Apple's App Store on iOS and Google Play on Android. The App never receives payment information.

5. Sharing

When you share a result or fall card, the App opens the system share sheet. The image and text go only to the destination you choose; the developer does not receive them.

6. Disclosure to third parties

The developer does not collect user information or provide it to third parties, except for the collection by the advertising service in section 3.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated as needed. Changes take effect when posted on this page.
